Our Story...

We first met on 2nd May, the day she came looking for a room for rent in our home village. Funny enough, the room she found was where a boy lived with the same name as mine—just kidding, obviously it was me 😄

That day was the first time I saw her, and she saw me too. She just kept staring at me like I was some kind of alien 👽😂. I got shy, acted normal, and went straight into my room. After that, life went on peacefully, like nothing special had happened… or so I thought.

One day, a girl messaged me on Instagram. Her name was Shanaya. The messages were simple—she said she liked me and studied in my school. After a few days, I noticed something strange: her messages only came before 8 AM in the morning and after 8 PM at night. That’s when I realized she was using her father’s phone, messaging me only when he was home.

A few days later, one of her friends confessed something to me—that she liked me.
That same evening, I saw her in the gallery of the house, combing her hair. I casually went up to her and said,
“I got to know something… from somewhere.”
She reacted immediately, “WHAT???” 😳
I smiled and said, “Just wait,” and walked away like I knew nothing.

The very next night, she confessed her love 💕
And just like that, we were officially in a relationship.

After one week, fear took over me. I thought, “These things aren’t good for me, I’m still a kid.” So I broke up with her.
But life had other plans.

She called me to the terrace. She grabbed my hand, looked straight into my eyes—tears rolling down, eyes red, face innocent—and asked just one word:
“Why?” 😢

I swear, I had never seen a girl like her in my life. I was shocked, confused, emotional… I didn’t know what to do. And without thinking, I just said,
“Yes.”

And there she was again—back in my life—like God Himself had pushed her into my destiny 🙏✨

On 2nd August, we had our first kiss 💋
Later, we joined the same tuition, had endless fun, laughter, and memories. We passed our 11th standard together.

In 12th, we took admission in her mother’s tuition, traveled in the same van, fought, laughed, annoyed each other, loved deeply, and lived almost 14 hours a day together. Life moved at the speed of light ⚡.

We passed 12th… but then came the twist.

She failed her 12th class.
I took a drop year for NDA, and she started working in digital marketing. Slowly, our conversations reduced—from daily talks to once in days… sometimes weeks. Our bond weakened. Small, unnecessary fights turned the relationship toxic 💔.

On 21st December, she said “I love you” for the first time.
On 19th January, we broke up.
On 22nd January, I had my JEE exam—and honestly, it destroyed me.

For the next 6–7 months, I didn’t know where I was going or what I was doing. I was just sleeping… existing… lost.

Then I got into a college in Gorakhpur, and life slowly started moving again.

On 21st December, the same date when she once said “I love you,” she said it again—this time, for the last time.
I messaged her with a silly excuse—I said I wanted a paneer recipe 🧀😂.
And just like that… we started talking again.

Today is 31st December.
And from this New Year, we are starting our relationship once again—with hope, maturity, and a heart full of lessons ❤️✨

Some love stories don’t end…
They pause, they break, they heal, and then they restart stronger 🌙💫
